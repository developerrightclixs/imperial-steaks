About Us

About Imperial Steaks

Imperial Steaks is an independent importer / distributor whose aim is to be the leading provider of top quality raw thin-sliced beef and chicken Philly steaks. Built on 25 years of restaurant-industry experience, Imperial Steaks and its team are dedicated to transferring their knowledge and expertise. Our vision is to work closely and partner with a diverse range of customers, including distributors, restaurants, retail, institutions and food service operators across Canada. Our commitment is to provide them with premium steak products that will help their business grow. We also offer Halal certified Philly steaks for the ever-changing demographics of our country, which in turn reaches a wider target market.

Quality, pricing and fit

Quality & pricing

Imperial Steaks is dedicated to offering competitive pricing and premium quality that will help us establish long-term relationships with our customers. Our aim is to work side-by-side with our customers, treat them as partners, and address all their needs and specifications.

Collaborating and being the exclusive importer / distributor in Canada and abroad with a trusted and dominant USA-based protein supplier that has served the industry for more than 100 years will enable us to build and differentiate ourselves from other brands.

Ideal for your establishment

  • PORTION CONTROLLED for ease of use and allowing you to track and account for the exact cost per serving.
  • COST-EFFECTIVE given our pricing competitiveness, which will reduce overall food costs.
  • PERFECT ALTERNATIVE to sliced rib eye, which is more expensive, less tasty and less tender than our product.
  • MENU-FRIENDLY allowing easy and quick meals, without compromising taste.

Whether you already serve thin-sliced beef or chicken or are thinking of adding it, we can work with you to enhance your menu and show you the profit-building potential of our products.
