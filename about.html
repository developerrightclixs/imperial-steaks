About Imperial Steaks

Imperial Steaks is a Philly steak importer and distributor of raw, thinly sliced beef and chicken. Built on 25 years of restaurant-industry experience, we partner with distributors, restaurants, retailers and foodservice operators across Canada, delivering exceptional flavour, consistent quality and dependable service. Our vision is to build lasting customer relationships through a commitment to quality and consistency, working with a supplier that has served the industry for more than 100 years.

Quality, pricing and fit

Quality & pricing

We aim to offer competitive pricing while delivering dependable quality and service. Our supplier operates USDA-inspected facilities and holds SQF Level 3 certification, which gives us a strong foundation for product quality and consistency. Collaborating with a supplier with over 100 years experience in the industry we are committed in establishing long-term relationship with all our customers by providing them exceptional value, quality, service, and integrity.

Ideal for your establishment

  • Cost-effective — reduces overall food costs
  • A tender, better-value alternative to sliced rib eye
  • Menu-friendly — quick to serve, without compromising taste

Whether you already serve thin-sliced beef or chicken or are thinking of adding it, we can work with you to enhance your menu and show you the profit-building potential of our products.
