Imperial Steaks — for client selection

Logo concepts

Three directions. In all of them IMPERIAL is the dominant word and STEAKS is the quiet one — the contrast the Tasty Steaks mark used, with the dominance of the two words reversed. Each is shown on matte black, on the brand red and on white, plus at the actual size it appears in the site header.

Pick one, or ask for changes to any of them. Every concept is vector artwork, so it scales to a carton or a billboard without loss.

01 — Imperial Serif

Bodoni Moda Black over a red hairline, with STEAKS set small and widely tracked underneath. The most traditional and the closest in feel to the original mark. Reads premium; needs a little more clear space than the other two.

02 — Block

Oswald Bold at full width, with STEAKS reversed out of a solid red bar beneath it. The most industrial of the three, and the one that survives being stamped on a carton or embroidered. Holds up smallest. This is the concept currently in place on the review build.
